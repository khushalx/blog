## The Starting Question

What makes a banking franchise valuable when funding becomes more expensive? This research outline uses HDFC Bank as the subject for a future study. It does not assess current results or make a recommendation.

Loan growth is visible. The quality and stability of the funding that supports it can be harder to evaluate. A useful analysis would follow deposits by type, funding costs, loan yields and credit losses over a consistent period.

## A Framework for the Work

The first task is to reconcile reported net interest margins with the mix of assets and liabilities. The second is to examine whether growth is changing the risk carried on the balance sheet.

- How much funding comes from stable customer relationships?
- What happens to earnings if deposits reprice faster than loans?
- Do reported credit costs reflect a full lending cycle?
- Are returns supported by sustainable underwriting or unusually favourable conditions?

<ResearchCallout title="Research discipline">
  For a bank, rapid growth is an observation. It becomes evidence of value
  creation only after considering funding, capital and credit quality.
</ResearchCallout>

## What Comes Next

A full note would build a historical series of deposits, advances, capital ratios and credit costs using dated annual reports and regulatory disclosures. It would explicitly distinguish consolidated and standalone figures.
