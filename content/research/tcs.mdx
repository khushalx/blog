## The Starting Question

How does an IT services business capture value when clients can automate more of the work? This outline identifies questions for a future study of Tata Consultancy Services. It does not claim to forecast the impact of AI on the company.

The research challenge is to separate a change in the tools used to deliver work from a change in the customer’s willingness to pay. Faster delivery could improve economics, reduce billable effort, expand demand, or produce some combination of all three.

## Business Economics to Examine

Revenue per employee is a useful starting point, but it needs context. Utilisation, subcontracting, offshore delivery, pricing and the complexity of engagements can all affect the figure.

A full study would distinguish time-based billing from fixed-price and outcome-based engagements. It would also ask how productivity improvements are shared between the service provider and its client.

<ResearchQuestion>
  If a project takes fewer hours, who captures the benefit: the client, the
  service provider, or both?
</ResearchQuestion>

## What I’m Watching

1. Evidence of changed contract structures, rather than broad statements about AI adoption.
2. Cash conversion alongside reported earnings.
3. The cost of training and redeploying employees.
4. Renewal behaviour and the durability of large client relationships.
